# Phase 6.6 — Print-Fit & Assembly Tools

Updated 2026-11-05. Target: Quest 3 / Touch Plus / Quest Browser. Hardware acceptance is pending.

## Parts and editing

Desktop: **Assembly** in the left sidebar. VR: **World Settings → Measure & Precision → Primitives → Assembly / Fit**. The primary action is **Check fit**: activate it, then tap two layers in the scene (or their names in the desktop Layers list). No printable-part setup is required for inspection. The second tap runs the check automatically and desktop results appear over the canvas. Escape or Cancel exits selection. Separate collapsed sections contain **Fit settings & accuracy**, **Peg & socket**, **Alignment**, and **Parts & export**. Cut remains directly on the main Tool Wheel. Choose an existing layer and define it as a printable part. Its name, selection, world/parent pose, visibility and locking use the existing Layers controls. Assigning another object to Part A reparents it while preserving world pose. A part contains its marked root and hierarchy descendants, including groups; nested printable parts are rejected. Detach an object or remove a part boundary without deleting its geometry.

Parts have separate authoritative fields. Member ordering, legacy layer Boolean operations, arrays and live Boolean stacks are evaluated within each boundary. Cross-part Boolean source references, including disabled edges, are rejected. Unassigned operands remain usable by a part; shared unassigned operands deliberately affect every consumer. Mark/clear/clearance changes, membership and mating use history. Root transforms move their children through the existing hierarchy.

**Show assembly preview**, beside Check fit, builds separate evaluated meshes for visible parts. **Show source layers** returns to editing. Selecting a layer retains the evaluated view; source edits invalidate it. Display caches use a 128³ cap and existing memory accounting. Only view intent is saved in optional display preferences; opening a project or recovery rebuilds derived meshes without changing source geometry or history. Legacy projects default to source view and can use the same preview button. A failed rebuild opens editable sources and reports a retry instruction.

## Fit inspection

Choose **Check fit**, then tap two different layers. Existing part members resolve to their printable root; a unique consumed Boolean source resolves to its result. Ordinary objects include their hierarchy descendants and Boolean dependencies, using detached metadata over the same source fields. Hidden layers, repeated taps on the first layer and parent/child pairs are rejected. Visible locked layers can be inspected. Inspection creates no part definitions, voxel changes or Undo actions. **Fit settings & accuracy → Desired gap** supplies the current check threshold without changing geometry or stored part clearances. The existing agent `part.inspect` command still uses the larger saved part clearance. A fixed 32³ lattice probes the shared signed-distance evaluation, projects nearby surface probes with SDF gradients, samples each against the other part, and reports approximate separation, sampled overlap, clearance violation and effective inspection/geometry resolution. At most 32 candidate collision locations are returned and drawn as transient red points. They identify sampled regions, not complete intersection boundaries or penetration depth.

Thin features, narrow collisions and the true closest pair can be missed. A missing reliable surface probe reports unknown separation. “No sampled overlap” is not proof of clearance. Requested clearance below effective resolution produces an explicit warning. Results become stale after scene revisions; they are never recomputed each frame. The fixed sample budget, validated Boolean depth/work quota and cooperative pauses bound work; cancellation and source changes reject stale output. These checks cannot guarantee physical fit, shrinkage, printer calibration or material behavior.

## Alignment and connectors

Choose centres, bounding faces or local axes; A moves toward B. Choose a model-world X/Y/Z positioning axis and a signed millimetre offset. Matching opposite local normals is optional. Use **Pick two layers** inside Alignment or Peg & socket to choose the pair interactively. Alignment and connector generation establish missing printable roots within their undo transaction; merely choosing the pair remains read-only. The headless mate API also accepts two explicit model-world surface normals, such as normals supplied by the existing snapping path. Rotations use minimal-twist quaternion alignment and preserve scale/source dimensions. World poses convert through the existing parent transform setter and form one undo action. Existing interactive surface snapping is unchanged.

Face alignment uses conservative world-aligned source bounds, not selected trimmed CAD faces: transformed primitive bounds are exact for boxes; curved shapes, sculpt lattice bounds, live CSG intersections and array grids can be conservative. Array bounds include existing grid padding. Rotation does not recompute a second contact constraint. Use the established surface snapping controls for direct contact placement and inspect the final result.

The connector generator creates an editable cylinder peg, unioned into A, and a cylinder socket cutter, subtracted from B. Dimensions, position and XYZ rotation are explicit mm/radians in the API; UI rotation is in degrees. Cylinder local Y is its axis and the position is its centre. The socket diameter must be at least peg diameter + twice radial clearance; depth must accommodate length. Diameter remains explicit: increasing clearance alone does not silently resize a previously entered socket diameter. Place the peg so it intersects its owning body and enters the other body; the generator does not guess a contact point or force connectedness.

Generation is one compound Undo/Redo action. Failure/cancellation rolls back completed source, parenting and modifier edits. Generated sources remain editable in Primitives and exact Transform. Duplicate them and use existing radial/linear arrays where compatible; apply matching settings to both sources for repeated matching connectors. Arrays do not automatically link their independent settings. Bake remains an explicit existing operation; changing Boolean references after baking is an explicit editing choice. Existing eight-layer, primitive aspect-ratio, voxel, sample and memory limits apply; a connector needs two free layer slots.

## STL and projects

**Export A STL + print report** or **Export visible parts separately** evaluates each boundary independently, including its reachable CSG operands. Fast, Standard and High quality use the existing export optimisers. Standard desktop current/all STL buttons also route marked-part projects to part export; explicit **Export unified boolean** retains its deliberate whole-scene union behavior. Part exports always use the project calibration in mm, preserving aspect ratio and physical dimensions. Each STL is recentered independently, consistent with the existing STL encoder. Part positions/orientations remain in the saved project; separate recentered STL files are not assembly placement files.

Final binary STL topology checks remain: boundary/non-manifold/inconsistent edges, degeneracy, connected surfaces, winding and existing unsupported checks. Blocking reports prevent UI downloads. Companion print reports retain warnings; inspect each file in the target slicer. Evaluated union inside a part is not a promise that disconnected members become one connected shell. References, controller models and editing overlays are not geometry inputs. Multiple parts are never silently unioned by the new part export workflow.

Exports refuse over-cap geometry or the existing 300,000-triangle/256 MiB validation budget. Fine connectors on much larger bodies can exceed these limits even when each source is small. Increase feature sizes or reduce bounds; no silent quality reduction occurs. Batch export is sequential and may have already downloaded earlier successful files when a later part fails. Browser download permissions can affect multiple-file delivery; verify downloads on Quest.

Native `.form` v14 stores a small marker/clearance on each part root. Membership uses the existing versioned hierarchy, avoiding a second reference graph. Projects without parts retain their prior version. Library / Save As / portable project duplication remaps existing object/hierarchy/Boolean IDs; autosave/recovery use the same codec. Prior native versions remain readable; older applications cannot open v14. Invalid markers, nested parts, cycles, missing references and cross-part Boolean graphs are refused before installation.

## Interactive selection follow-up — 2026-11-05

The main desktop disclosure has one primary button, with the current selection/result directly underneath it. There are no A/B dropdowns or manual source cycling in the main flow. The headset root page has Check fit, desired gap, pair picking and three explicit subpages rather than the prior 14-operation grid. Existing advanced actions remain in their contextual sections.

Scene taps are captured before desktop gizmo/sculpt/Tube input. Layers-list name clicks share the same picker, including keyboard activation. Tool-hand trigger selection uses release-to-arm and retains press ownership through the release after the second tap; menu hover suspends picking, while tracking loss, hand/controller reassignment and project replacement cancel partial selection. Both hands use the same ray path. Outlines are transient, hover is limited to 20 Hz, fit is calculated only on completion, and revision/cancellation guards reject stale asynchronous results. Desktop controls refresh while a project/dialog busy state changes; this also prevents the previously stale disabled state after opening a project. Physical Quest validation of the new interaction remains pending.

## Surface connector placement follow-up — 2026-11-06

**Peg & socket → Place peg & socket** now starts a three-tap workflow: peg layer, socket layer, joint face. Ordinary layers remain unmarked until the final creation action. Diameter, total peg cylinder length and radial clearance are the basic controls; socket diameter defaults to peg diameter plus twice radial clearance and socket cylinder depth to peg length + 0.5 mm. Editing Advanced socket sizes intentionally disables automatic sizing until **Use matching socket sizes** is chosen. Both cylinders are centred on the sampled faces: half the peg length is embedded and half the socket cylinder depth enters the second solid. The numeric centre/orientation workflow remains available in Advanced.

During joint placement the socket layer is temporarily represented by an outline so it cannot obscure the first layer’s joint face. This is a rendering override, never a saved visibility edit. Live wire cylinders follow the first surface and the matching facing surface along its outward normal; their axes align automatically. Parts can be separated: the peg and cutter receive independent world centres, parent-aware local transforms, and remain in their original part positions. No arbitrary movement/rotation of the two bodies is performed. Faces must oppose within 60 degrees and the second surface must lie along the outward axis. Misses, removed CSG material, locked layers, overlapping bodies and rays outside reach are refused. Near edges, very thin features and difficult fields can fail the bounded trace; no exact CAD or physical-fit guarantee is implied.

Surface fields reuse the shared evaluated SDF, including hierarchy transforms, live Booleans and arrays. Each face trace is bounded to 96 advancement steps plus normal-gradient samples, with finite bounds and cached prepared fields; hover remains capped at 20 Hz and creates no remesh. Workspace transformations are removed before querying and model-mm calibration is explicit. Preview geometry is reused and excluded from exports. Escape/Cancel, controller changes/tracking loss before confirmation, and project replacement discard uncommitted placement. After confirmation the ordinary generator owns one atomic transaction. Cancelling before publication rolls it back; cancelling the later derived-preview build leaves the explicitly created connector and reports that Undo remains available.

On successful interactive creation, an evaluated independent assembly preview is prepared automatically. Source creation and preview are reported separately: a resource/quality refusal cannot masquerade as a rolled-back creation. Existing eight-layer, sample, memory and topology limits remain. Both desktop and VR expose only the three sizing fields and one placement action by default; Advanced retains separate socket sizes and numeric placement. Physical Quest 3 three-tap comfort, ghost visibility, controller interruptions and actual printed/slicer fit remain pending.

### Bounded assembly display (2026-11-06)

Assembly preview uses an independent 128³ maximum display cache per part. Reaching its target-detail cap no longer refuses an otherwise valid preview: successful feedback reports the effective millimetre detail when capped. Memory/sample/triangle limits and stale/cancel checks still apply. This lets default 50 mm boxes display the stock 4 mm peg and matching socket. The parametric source dimensions remain exact; display sampling is approximate. Part STL export retains its existing stricter quality checks and can still refuse a capped evaluated mesh.

## Preview recovery and export reliability (2026-11-06)

Boolean exports acquire a nested memory lease: stop background recovery work, settle and evict the global Boolean display cache, and release independent assembly display meshes. Source fields stay editable and unchanged. Sequential batch export holds one outer lease, then restores the prior evaluated preview. Source revisions ignore selection but still reject modelling changes and cancellation.

A dedicated export compositor samples analytical primitives from their smallest feature dimension (ten samples across); sculpt fields retain native spacing. STL skips colour caches. Each chunk receives conservative planar simplification before admission to the whole-model topology budget; chunk boundaries and curved vertices remain fixed. Physical Float32 conversion can produce an exactly collinear bridge triangle: the encoder retriangulates its unique neighbour using the existing middle vertex, without moving vertices or welding nearby surfaces. Ambiguous cases remain blocking. The final topology validator is unchanged. Reports record effective sampling spacing, removed planar triangles, maximum planar deviation and precision retriangulations. Existing quality, sample, memory and 300,000-triangle caps remain enforced.

Host stock fixture: two default 50 mm boxes, 4 mm diameter / 6 mm peg and 0.3 mm radial clearance, with an active assembly preview. High exports produced 96,900 and 75,526 triangles (4.85 and 3.78 MB), with zero boundary, non-manifold, inconsistent or degenerate triangles; dimensions approximately 53 × 50 × 50 mm and 50 × 50 × 50 mm. Planar deviation was zero. Host export times were 19.39 s and 14.93 s; these are individual CPU observations, not headset performance or physical print acceptance.
